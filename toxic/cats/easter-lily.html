Toxic to cats: Easter Lily

Trumpet lily (Lilium longiflorum)

Kibakibascan.com

Save image Save for TikTok

Easter lily, also called trumpet lily or white lily, is the plant Lilium longiflorum. Every part of the plant, including the pollen and the water in the vase, is toxic to cats and can cause kidney failure; cats are the only species known to be affected.

Signs reported in cats

  • Vomiting
  • Dull, quiet behavior (depression)
  • Not eating (anorexia)
  • Drinking and urinating more (polyuria, polydipsia)
  • Dehydration
  • Little or no urine (oliguria/anuria)
  • Kidney changes shown in blood and urine tests (isosthenuria, azotemia)
  • Damage to the pancreas (pancreatic acinar degeneration)
  • Seizures
