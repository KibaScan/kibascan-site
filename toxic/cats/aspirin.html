Toxic to cats: Aspirin

Over-the-counter pain reliever

Found in

Bayer Genuine Aspirin · Ecotrin Regular Strength · Excedrin Extra Strength · Alka-Seltzer Original

Kibakibascan.com

Save image Save for TikTok

Aspirin is sold as Bayer Genuine Aspirin and Ecotrin Regular Strength, and it is one of the pain relievers in Excedrin Extra Strength. Cats clear aspirin slowly, and doses of 325 mg twice a day have been lethal to cats.

Signs reported in cats

  • Vomiting, often with blood (vomiting, often hematemesis)
  • Not eating (anorexia)
  • Black, tarry stools (melena)
  • High body temperature (hyperthermia)
  • Fast breathing (tachypnea)
  • Acid build-up in the blood (metabolic acidosis)
  • Dull, quiet behavior (depression)
  • Loss of coordination (ataxia)
  • Stomach ulcers (gastric ulceration)
  • Liver injury (hepatic injury)
