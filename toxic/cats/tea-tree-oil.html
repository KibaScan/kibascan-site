Toxic to cats: Tea tree oil

Essential oil, also called melaleuca

Found in

Desert Essence 100% Australian Tea Tree Oil · doTERRA Tea Tree Essential Oil · Plant Therapy Tea Tree Essential Oil · Aura Cacia Tea Tree Essential Oil

Kibakibascan.com

Save image Save for TikTok

Tea tree oil is an essential oil sold in small bottles and added to shampoos and skin products. In a review of 443 cases in dogs and cats, signs followed concentrated tea tree oil put on the skin or swallowed, in amounts from 0.1 to 85 mL.

Signs reported in cats

  • Low body temperature (hypothermia)
  • Loss of coordination (ataxia)
  • Weakness or partial paralysis (paresis)
  • Tremors
  • Drooling (hypersalivation)
  • Coma
  • Raised liver enzymes in blood tests (elevated ALT/AST)
  • Death

Sources

  • Khan SA et al., JAVMA 2014 — Concentrated tea tree oil toxicosis in dogs and cats: 443 cases

  • Villar D et al., Vet Hum Toxicol 1994 — Toxicity of melaleuca oil in dogs and cats
