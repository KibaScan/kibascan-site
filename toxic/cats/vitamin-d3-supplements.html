Toxic to cats: Vitamin D3 Supplements

Vitamin D pills and psoriasis medicines

Found in

Nature Made Vitamin D3 2000 IU Softgels · NatureWise Vitamin D3 · Nature's Bounty Vitamin D3 5000 IU Softgels · Taclonex Topical Suspension

Vitamin D3, also called cholecalciferol, raises calcium and phosphorus in the blood of animals that swallow too much, which can leave mineral deposits in soft tissues and injure the kidneys. The list below also includes prescription psoriasis medicines made with the vitamin D relatives calcipotriene and calcitriol.

Signs reported in cats

  • Drinking and urinating more (polydipsia, polyuria)
  • Not eating (anorexia)
  • Vomiting
  • Low energy (lethargy)
  • Weakness
  • Dehydration
  • High calcium and phosphorus in the blood (hypercalcemia, hyperphosphatemia)
  • Raised kidney values in blood tests (azotemia)
  • Mineral deposits in soft tissues (soft-tissue mineralization)
  • Kidney failure (renal failure)
