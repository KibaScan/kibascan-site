Toxic to cats: Minoxidil

Hair-regrowth foam or liquid

Found in

Men's Rogaine Minoxidil · Women's Rogaine Minoxidil · Hims Minoxidil · Keeps Minoxidil

Kibakibascan.com

Save image Save for TikTok

Minoxidil is the hair-regrowth drug in foams and liquids that people put on the scalp. Cats are more sensitive to it than dogs, and cats have been poisoned after lying on a pillow with minoxidil residue and then grooming themselves.

Signs reported in cats

  • Low energy (lethargy)
  • Not eating (anorexia)
  • Vomiting
  • Fast or labored breathing (tachypnea, dyspnea)
  • Low blood pressure (hypotension)
  • Fast heart rate (tachycardia)
  • Low body temperature (hypothermia)
  • Fluid around or in the lungs (pleural effusion, pulmonary edema)
  • Blue or gray gums (cyanosis)
  • Heart failure (congestive heart failure)
