Toxic to cats: Acetaminophen

Pain and fever reliever

Found in

Tylenol Extra Strength · Tylenol Regular Strength · Vicks NyQuil Cold & Flu · Excedrin Extra Strength

Kibakibascan.com

Save image Save for TikTok

Acetaminophen is the pain and fever reliever in Tylenol Extra Strength and in cold and flu medicines such as Vicks NyQuil Cold & Flu. Cats have a limited ability to break it down, and Merck Veterinary Manual reports poisoning in cats at 40–50 mg/kg, with signs in some cats at 10 mg/kg.

Signs reported in cats

  • Blue or gray gums (cyanosis)
  • Brown or muddy-colored gums (brown/muddy mucous membranes)
  • Swollen face or paws (facial/paw edema)
  • Drooling (hypersalivation)
  • Vomiting
  • Low energy (lethargy)
  • Fast breathing (tachypnea)
  • Low body temperature (hypothermia)
  • Yellow gums, skin or eyes (hepatic icterus)
