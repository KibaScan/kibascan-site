Toxic to cats: Ibuprofen

Over-the-counter pain reliever

Found in

Advil Tablets · Motrin IB · Advil Liqui-Gels · Advil PM

Kibakibascan.com

Save image Save for TikTok

Ibuprofen is the pain reliever in Advil Tablets and Motrin IB. Cats are about twice as sensitive to it as dogs, and ibuprofen poisoning in pets can cause stomach ulcers and kidney damage.

Signs reported in cats

  • Vomiting, sometimes with blood (vomiting, hematemesis)
  • Black, tarry stools (melena)
  • Not eating (anorexia)
  • Abdominal pain
  • Drinking and urinating more (polydipsia/polyuria)
  • Kidney injury (azotemia, acute kidney injury)
  • Loss of coordination (ataxia)
  • Seizures
  • A hole in the stomach wall (gastric perforation)
