Toxic to dogs: Naproxen

Over-the-counter pain reliever

Found in

Aleve Tablets · Aleve Liquid Gels · Aleve PM · Aleve Back & Muscle Pain

Kibakibascan.com

Save image Save for TikTok

Naproxen is the pain reliever in Aleve Tablets. Its half-life in dogs is 74 hours, and the American College of Veterinary Pharmacists notes signs of poisoning in dogs at doses of 5 mg/kg.

Signs reported in dogs

  • Vomiting, sometimes with blood (vomiting, hematemesis)
  • Black, tarry stools (melena)
  • Not eating (anorexia)
  • Weakness
  • Pale gums (pale mucous membranes)
  • Urinating more than usual (polyuria)
  • Urinating very little (oliguria)
  • Raised kidney values in blood tests (azotemia)
