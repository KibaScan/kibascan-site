Toxic to dogs: Grapes

Table, wine and seedless grapes

Kibakibascan.com

Save image Save for TikTok

Eating grapes has been associated with vomiting and kidney failure in some dogs, and tartaric acid is one proposed cause. The amount of tartaric acid differs from grape to grape, so the effect of a given amount differs too.

Signs reported in dogs

  • Vomiting
  • Diarrhea
  • Low energy (lethargy)
  • Not eating (anorexia)
  • Abdominal pain
  • Drinking more than usual (polydipsia)
  • Little or no urine (oliguria, anuria)
  • Sudden kidney injury (acute kidney injury)

In dogs that develop it, kidney failure appears within 24 to 72 hours of eating them.

Sources

  • Wegenast et al. 2021 (JAVMA) — Tartaric Acid Hypothesis
