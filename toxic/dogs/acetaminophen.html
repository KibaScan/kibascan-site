Toxic to dogs: Acetaminophen

Pain and fever reliever

Found in

Tylenol Extra Strength · Tylenol Regular Strength · Vicks NyQuil Cold & Flu · Excedrin Extra Strength

Kibakibascan.com

Save image Save for TikTok

Acetaminophen is the pain and fever reliever in Tylenol Extra Strength and in cold and flu medicines such as Vicks NyQuil Cold & Flu. In dogs, liver damage tends to appear at lower doses and red blood cell changes at higher doses; Merck Veterinary Manual reports signs at doses above 100 mg/kg and methemoglobinemia, a change in the blood's oxygen-carrying pigment, above 200 mg/kg.

Signs reported in dogs

  • Vomiting
  • Low energy (lethargy)
  • Not eating (anorexia)
  • Yellow gums, skin or eyes (icterus)
  • Muddy-brown gums (muddy-brown mucous membranes)
  • Swollen face or paws (facial/paw edema)
  • Dark red or brown urine (hemoglobinuria)
  • Abdominal pain
