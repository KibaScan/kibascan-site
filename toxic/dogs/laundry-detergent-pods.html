Toxic to dogs: Laundry Detergent Pods

Single-dose packets of detergent

Examples

Tide PODS · Gain Flings · all free clear Mighty Pacs · Persil Ultra Pacs

Kibakibascan.com

Save image Save for TikTok

Laundry pods are single-dose packets of concentrated detergent. When a dog bites into one, the contents can be released under pressure, and the vomiting and inhaled foam that can follow make pods a more serious concern than ordinary laundry detergent.

Signs reported in dogs

  • Profuse vomiting
  • Drooling
  • Gagging
  • Coughing
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Wheezing
  • Foaming at the mouth
  • Low energy (lethargy)
  • Diarrhea
  • Oral and eye irritation
  • Weakness or collapse
