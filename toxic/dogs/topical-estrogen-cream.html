Toxic to dogs: Topical Estrogen Cream

Hormone gel, cream, spray or patch

Found in

EstroGel · Divigel · Evamist · Estrace Cream

Kibakibascan.com

Save image Save for TikTok

Estrogen gels, creams, sprays and patches are hormone medicines that people put on the skin or, for some creams, use vaginally. Dogs are typically exposed by licking the medicine from a person's skin or by chewing a used patch, and Merck Veterinary Manual reports that dogs that swallow 1 mg/kg or more can develop bone marrow suppression.

Signs reported in dogs

  • Swollen vulva
  • Bloody vaginal discharge
  • Signs of being in heat
  • Symmetrical hair loss
  • Enlarged mammary glands
  • Attracting male dogs
  • Lethargy and weakness
  • Pale gums
  • Bruising or pinpoint skin bleeding
  • Loss of coordination
  • Bone marrow that stops making enough blood cells (bone marrow suppression, aplastic anemia)
