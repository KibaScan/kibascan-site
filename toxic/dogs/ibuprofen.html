Toxic to dogs: Ibuprofen

Over-the-counter pain reliever

Found in

Advil Tablets · Motrin IB · Advil Liqui-Gels · Advil PM

Kibakibascan.com

Save image Save for TikTok

Ibuprofen is the pain reliever in Advil Tablets and Motrin IB, and stomach ulcers and kidney damage are the effects most often reported in dogs. A study of 434 dogs counted doses above 25 mg/kg as toxic to the stomach and gut and above 100 mg/kg as toxic to the kidneys, and Merck Veterinary Manual describes seizures and coma above 400 mg/kg.

Signs reported in dogs

  • Vomiting, sometimes with blood (vomiting, hematemesis)
  • Black, tarry stools (melena)
  • Not eating (anorexia)
  • Abdominal pain
  • Drinking and urinating more (polyuria, polydipsia)
  • Raised kidney values in blood tests (azotemia)
  • Loss of coordination (ataxia)
  • Seizures
