Toxic to dogs: Vitamin D3 supplements

Vitamin D pills and psoriasis medicines

Found in

Nature Made Vitamin D3 2000 IU Softgels · NatureWise Vitamin D3 · Nature's Bounty Vitamin D3 5000 IU Softgels · Taclonex Topical Suspension

Vitamin D3, also called cholecalciferol, raises calcium in a dog's blood when too much is swallowed, and kidney injury can follow. The list below also includes prescription psoriasis medicines made with the vitamin D relatives calcipotriene and calcitriol.

Signs reported in dogs

  • Vomiting
  • Not eating (anorexia)
  • Drinking and urinating more (polyuria, polydipsia)
  • Low energy (lethargy)
  • Black, tarry stools (melena)
  • Slow heart rate (bradycardia)
  • Mineral deposits in soft tissues (soft-tissue mineralization)
  • Sudden kidney injury (acute kidney injury)
