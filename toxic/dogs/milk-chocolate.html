Use caution with dogs: Milk chocolate

Chocolate bars, chips and candy

Kibakibascan.com

Save image Save for TikTok

Milk chocolate contains theobromine and caffeine, and Merck Veterinary Manual puts it at about 64 mg of the two per ounce. Merck reports that mild signs may occur in dogs at 20 mg of these per kg of body weight, and describes about 1 ounce of milk chocolate per pound of body weight as potentially lethal.

Signs reported in dogs

  • Vomiting
  • Diarrhea
  • Drinking more than usual (polydipsia)
  • Hyperactivity
  • Fast heart rate (tachycardia)
  • Tremors (at higher doses)

Sources

  • Merck Veterinary Manual — Chocolate Toxicosis
  • ASPCA APCC — Methylxanthines
  • PetMD/Veterinary Poisons Information Service — Chocolate Doses
